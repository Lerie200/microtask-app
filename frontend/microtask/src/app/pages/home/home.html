NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Find answers to common questions about using MicroEarn.

What kind of tasks can I complete?

Depending on availability, tasks may include photo submissions, voice recordings, image selection, classification, and other simple data activities. Each task provides its own instructions.

How do I get paid?

Review the reward and requirements shown for each task. Submitted work may need approval before earnings are credited. Check your account for the current payment and withdrawal rules.

Is there an activation fee?

If your account requires activation, the applicable fee and payment instructions should be displayed before payment. Review these details carefully before confirming.

What do I need to get started?

You need an account, an internet connection, and a suitable device for the tasks you choose. Some tasks may require access to a phone camera, microphone, or M-Pesa.

Can I access my account later?

Yes. Use the Log In link to return to your account and access the features available to your account.

How do I get help?

Contact the MicroEarn support team using the verified contact details provided by the platform.
