Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of tasks will I do?

Simple, everyday actions like taking a photo, recording a short voice clip, or picking the right option from a few choices — all used to help train AI systems.

How do I get paid?

Each task shows its own reward amount upfront. Your submissions are reviewed, and approved tasks count toward your earnings, tracked right in your dashboard.

What do I need to get started?

Just a smartphone, a reliable internet connection, and an M-Pesa line for the one-time activation step.

Is my data safe?

Your photos and recordings are only used for the specific task you submit them for.
